# Cinematic Street Fashion Portrait

A moody nighttime street fashion portrait with neon lighting and motion blur.

## Settings
- **Lens:** 50mm f/1.2
- **Lighting:** Neon signs, street lamps, car headlights
- **Mood:** Edgy, urban, mysterious
- **Style:** Cyberpunk meets high fashion

## The Prompt

```
A cinematic street fashion portrait of a young model walking through
a rainy Tokyo alley at night. Wearing oversized black leather jacket
with neon orange accents, dark jeans, and chunky boots. Shot with
a 50mm f/1.2 lens, shallow depth of field. Background is filled
with Japanese neon signs in pink, blue, and orange creating beautiful
color bokeh. Rain is visible in the air, creating light streaks.
Motion blur on the model suggests movement. Wet pavement reflects
all the neon colors. Color grading is cinematic with deep blacks,
teal shadows, and orange highlights. Shot by a fashion photographer
for Vogue Japan. Cyberpunk aesthetic meets high fashion editorial.
Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, hyper-detailed, 8K quality.
```
