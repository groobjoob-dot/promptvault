## Prompt

```
Use the uploaded portrait/face image as the identity reference for the main person. Preserve the person's exact facial identity, facial structure, age, hairstyle, beard/facial hair, skin texture and recognizable features. Do not beautify, retouch, redesign or alter the identity.

Create an ultra-realistic cinematic advertising photograph of a gigantic premium modern smartphone standing vertically in the middle of a sophisticated urban city environment, surrounded by tall glass skyscrapers with warm illuminated windows during blue hour / early evening.

The smartphone is enormous, occupying almost the entire vertical frame, slightly tilted in perspective, with realistic metallic edges, polished dark titanium frame, subtle reflections and physically accurate glass reflections. The camera is positioned slightly below the phone, creating a dramatic monumental perspective.

On the smartphone screen, recreate a premium dark-mode Google-style search homepage inspired by the reference image.

At the top center of the screen: the colorful Google logo.

Below the logo, place the referenced person sitting cross-legged directly inside the smartphone screen, facing the camera. The person is wearing an elegant dark navy/black suit with a crisp white shirt and white sneakers. He is sitting comfortably with a realistic confident expression.

Place TWO realistic gray tabby cats sitting naturally beside him, one on each side, both facing toward the camera. The cats must have highly detailed realistic fur, natural anatomy, expressive eyes and believable interaction with the person.

The person is holding an open dark premium laptop on his lap, with realistic proportions, reflections and subtle Apple-style laptop aesthetics without unnecessary branding emphasis.

Below the person and cats, recreate the search interface:
A large rounded dark search bar containing the Hebrew text:
"קהילת הפרומפטים"

Include a colorful Google-style search icon on the left side of the search bar and microphone / visual search icons on the right.

Below it, add realistic dark-mode search buttons:
"Google Search"
"I'm Feeling Lucky"

Below the buttons:
"Google offered in: עברית"

Further down, create a realistic search result card with:
"קהילת הפרומפטים | Nano Banana"

and a smaller Hebrew description:
"קהילה ליצירת פרומפטים, תמונות, וידאו ובינה מלאכותית"

Include subtle category/navigation buttons underneath with Hebrew labels such as:
"פרומפטים"
"קהילה"
"תמונות"
"וידאו"

At the very bottom of the smartphone screen, include a dark navigation bar with:
"All   Images   Videos   News   Maps   More"

Maintain the exact visual hierarchy and approximate placement of all interface elements from the reference image.

IMPORTANT:
The smartphone screen must feel like a real functional interface integrated into the physical phone, not a flat graphic pasted onto it.
The person, cats and laptop must appear physically inside the screen with realistic depth and cinematic lighting.

Style:
ultra-photorealistic, premium commercial advertising photography, cinematic lighting, realistic glass reflections, physically accurate materials, HDR, extremely detailed skin and hair, realistic cat fur, sharp focus on the smartphone and subject, shallow depth of field in the background, subtle bokeh, volumetric city lighting, high dynamic range, dramatic perspective, sophisticated dark color palette, premium technology campaign aesthetic, photorealistic 3D integration, 8K detail.

vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, centered composition, gigantic smartphone dominating the frame, full phone visible from top to bottom, slightly low camera angle, symmetrical subject placement, cinematic depth, realistic scale.

Do not change the person's identity from the reference image.
Do not add extra people.
Do not distort hands, face, cats or laptop.
Do not create duplicated objects.
Do not add random text.
No watermark.
No unnecessary logos.
No borders.
No white strips.
No poster frame.
```
