# Ultra-Realistic Editorial Male Portrait

Create an ultra-realistic editorial portrait of a man in natural daylight. Use a medium format camera look with shallow depth of field.

## Settings
- **Lens:** 85mm f/1.4
- **Lighting:** Soft window light from the left, golden hour warmth
- **Mood:** Confident, contemplative
- **Framing:** Head and shoulders, slight 3/4 angle

## The Prompt

```
Create an ultra-realistic editorial portrait of a 32-year-old man with
short dark hair and light stubble. He is wearing a crisp white linen
shirt, unbuttoned at the collar. Shot on medium format film, 85mm
lens, f/1.4 aperture, creating creamy bokeh. Natural daylight from a
large window on the left side, golden hour warmth mixing with cool
shadows. His expression is calm and confident, looking slightly past
the camera. Background is a minimalist interior with soft beige tones,
slightly out of focus. Skin texture is natural and detailed - pores,
subtle imperfections, realistic subsurface scattering. Color grading
is clean with warm midtones and cool shadows. Editorial magazine
quality, shot by a fashion photographer. Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio.
```

## Tips

- **Best with:** Midjourney v6, Flux Pro, GPT Image 2
- **Variations:** Change age, hair, outfit for different looks
- **Style:** Add "Vogue editorial" or "GQ cover" for more impact
- **Resolution:** Use 2K or higher for print quality
