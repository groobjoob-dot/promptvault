# Stylized 3D Pixar Character Portrait

A friendly 3D character in the iconic Pixar animation style.

## Settings
- **Style:** Pixar Animation Studios aesthetic
- **Lighting:** Soft studio lighting with rim light
- **Mood:** Warm, friendly, approachable
- **Rendering:** Octane Render quality

## The Prompt

```
A stylized 3D character portrait in the style of Pixar Animation
Studios. The character is a friendly 28-year-old man with round
glasses, a warm smile, and messy brown hair. He's wearing a cozy
navy blue sweater and a white t-shirt underneath. The character
has exaggerated Pixar features - large expressive eyes, smooth
skin, slightly oversized head. Rendered in Octane with soft global
illumination, subsurface scattering on the skin, and a subtle
rim light creating separation from the background. Background is
a soft gradient from warm cream to peachy orange. The character
is centered, looking directly at the camera with a warm, inviting
expression. Pixar-quality rendering, hyper-detailed, 4K resolution,
cinematic lighting setup. Aspect ratio 1:1.
```

- **Best with:** Midjourney v6, Flux Pro
- **Character variations:** Change age, gender, outfit
- **Style:** Add "Dreamworks style" or "Illumination style" for variations
- **Mood:** Add "happy," "thoughtful," "surprised" for expressions
