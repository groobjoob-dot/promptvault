# Neon Wilderness Hiking Portrait

A striking neon-outdoor portrait with bold electric colors, technical hiking gear, and reflective golden mountain sunglasses against a stylized cyan forest

## Prompt

```
Create a striking neon-outdoor portrait illustration with a bold, high-end digital art aesthetic. Show the subject in a close chest-up side-profile composition, facing slightly upward and into the distance with a calm, focused, adventurous expression. Dress the subject in modern hiking-inspired outerwear: a technical jacket with layered collar detail, a visible backpack strap, and sleek reflective sunglasses. Add a stylish outdoor cap that feels contemporary and functional.

Use an intense electric color treatment built around hot magenta, crimson, deep violet, cyan, and teal. Sculpt the face, beard or facial contours, hair, clothing, and gear with dramatic color separation and smooth stylized shading. The sunglasses should reflect warm golden-orange tones, adding contrast and a premium feel.

Place the subject against a dark background with softly stylized vertical forest silhouettes or pine-tree shapes glowing in cool cyan-teal light. The environment should feel like a futuristic wilderness at night rather than a realistic photograph. Keep the background simple, graphic, and atmospheric so the character remains the clear focal point.

Render the image as polished digital artwork with crisp edges, rich contrast, vivid gradients, refined line control, and a premium poster-like finish. Keep the portrait clean, modern, and visually powerful. Maintain the refined creative standard through strong composition, excellent identity preservation, and elevated stylization.

The final image should feel bold, adventurous, stylish, and instantly eye-catching, like a luxury neon travel-art portrait.
```
